Welcome to OneMoreThing.

    
This tool listens to a pulmonary nodule consultation and shows you, at a glance, which key questions have been covered and which are still missing. It does not diagnose or score risk — it simply helps make sure nothing important is missed.

    

      
1
Name the consultation and press Start. The date and time are saved automatically.

      
2
Talk to your patient as normal. The tiles recolour as topics are covered: red = still to ask, amber = mentioned but unclear, green = captured, grey = not applicable. Glance up before finishing.

      
3
Press Stop. The transcript writes out, and you get a draft summary to copy into your notes. Paste the CT/PET report to complete the risk-score readiness.

    

    
The quickest way to see it working is to play the sample consultation.

    

      
      
    

    
Prototype for informal testing only. Not for real patient data. All clinical decisions remain the responsibility of the treating clinician.

  







  

    
Saved consultations.

    
Each consultation is saved on this computer when you press Stop, so you can come back to it at the end of the day. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

    

    

      
    

  







  

    
CT / PET report

    
Paste the CT and/or PET report text (or upload a .txt file). The radiology fields — nodule size, type, spiculation, location, count and PET uptake — are extracted automatically. You can review and edit any of them afterwards in the radiology panel.

    

      
    

    

    

    

      
      
      
    

    
Values are clinician/radiology-sourced and unlock the matching Brock/Herder rows. They are never inferred from the patient conversation.

AI refinement (optional)

    
By default OneMoreThing runs a fully offline rule-based engine — no key needed. You can optionally connect an OpenAI-compatible LLM to improve how ambiguous speech is mapped to checklist statuses. Your key stays in this browser only.
